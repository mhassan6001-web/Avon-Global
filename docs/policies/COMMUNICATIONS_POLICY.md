# AVEN GLOBAL — Staff communications and retention policy

Status: **retention periods proposed by the Director; legal confirmation pending.**

## What the system provides

- One-to-one and department messages, company announcements with optional
  acknowledgement, tasks with deadlines, morning work plans, end-of-day reports,
  and report requests from the Director.

## What the system does not do

- No hidden monitoring. The system does not record keystrokes, screens, location,
  idle time, microphone or camera, and does not read other people's messages in
  the background.
- Reports and plans contain only what the employee writes and submits.
- The Director's "Team today" view shows only counts that staff can see about
  themselves: open, overdue and completed tasks, and whether a plan or report was
  submitted.
- Read and acknowledgement markers are shown to the sender. Staff are told this
  in the interface ("Ask to acknowledge", "seen / not yet seen").

## Who can see what

- A conversation is visible only to its members. The Director is not silently
  added to staff conversations.
- Tasks are visible to the person who assigned them and the person assigned.
- Work reports are visible to the author and the Director.
- Every access is permission-checked in the backend and audited.

## Records are not silently changed

Messages, task updates and reports are append-only. A correction is a new message
or update. This keeps an honest record for both the company and the employee.

## Retention (proposed by the Director 2026-11-12; subject to Malawi legal, tax and privacy confirmation)

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| Financial records, invoices, receipts and supporting documents | 7 years minimum, longer if the law requires |
| Audit logs | 7 years minimum |
| Official business reports that support financial or legal records | 7 years |
| Ordinary internal messages | 2 years by default |
| Employee and HR records | As employment and privacy law requires |

Retention will be configurable. No automatic deletion is implemented yet.
Archival and authorised deletion will be built only after legal confirmation,
and will never touch records under a legal hold or investigation.

## Employee notice

Staff should be given this policy before using the system. JARVIS-prepared
messages and tasks are labelled "prepared by JARVIS".
