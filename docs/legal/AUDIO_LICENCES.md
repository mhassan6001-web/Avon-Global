# Audio licences

Every sound AVEN GLOBAL plays must be recorded here before it ships.

## Interface sounds (Stage 3)

| Sound events | Source | Author | Licence | Notes |
|--------------|--------|--------|---------|-------|
| startup, shutdown, navigation, module, typing, notification, message, approval, complete, warning, urgent, jarvisOn, jarvisOff, listening, processing, response | Synthesised at run time by `frontend/src/lib/sound/recipes.ts` (Web Audio oscillators and filtered noise) | AVEN GLOBAL project code | Original work of the project; no third-party audio | No recordings, samples or film/game audio are used or imitated. |

## JARVIS voice (Stage 12, not yet chosen)

The spoken voice will come from a text-to-speech provider. Before it ships,
record here: provider, voice name or ID, the commercial-use terms, and
confirmation that the voice does not imitate an actor or character.

## Wake word (Stage 12)

Picovoice Porcupine, paid licence to be supplied by the Director. The custom
wake-word model file and its licence terms go here once issued.
